# Session UI

- The compact Chats project index appears while scrolling over the full-width list, accepts direct tap/scrub only inside its visible opaque rail, and resolves jumps against committed rendered sections. Never attach an index long-press recognizer to the list. Linger dismissal must be invalidated by renewed scrolling/touch, snapshot replacement, backgrounding and detachment; search and other groupings retain their existing behavior.

- Project grouping may hide the repeated place label, but must retain raw repository identity for the index. Keep index artwork at 24 pt in targets with at least 48 pt pitch; overflow uses bounded landmarks instead of squeezing targets. Keep landmarks fixed during a gesture and retain every destination through scrubbing and accessibility. Anchor scrubbing to the tapped landmark so small finger movement cannot jump to an unrelated proportional destination. The rail background must remain opaque in both light and dark appearances.

- Index entrance and exit use restrained movement with full opacity at rest. Disable hit targets when dismissal begins, fence interrupted animation completions, and skip movement under Reduce Motion. Lifecycle cancellation must stop animations and hide immediately.

- Operation receipts and the relations tree use compact conversation-list rows with provider marks, trailing status and curved child rails. Preserve separate reply and exact-target actions; receipt status belongs to its creating turn, while tree status follows the live catalog.
- Operation cards consume the public progress/completion/batch projection without rewriting receipts. Hide duplicate successful creation completions linked to progress; retain failed/cancelled targets, uncertain delivery and omitted-member counts. Navigation revalidates the live catalog and keeps the exact Tab target. Saved reply text remains readable after its target disappears.
- The composer relations capsule opens a catalog-backed tree. Containment owns Tabs: keep them in their conversation’s horizontal row, with hidden scrollbars and directional overflow fades. Only precise opened-by links add depth and curved rails between conversations. Require the precise opener and its root to exist, never route through `openedByRootSessionId` alone, exclude archived and side-panel targets, and guard cycles. Scope changes clear the tree and disable old navigation.

- Relation and receipt rows express containment through layout, without auxiliary Tab/current/directory labels. Mark only the current exact target with a subtle background and accessible selection. Keep status descriptions accessible, use icons instead of redundant visible status words, and preserve reply text, truncation/error notices and action labels.
