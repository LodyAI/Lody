# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LoroHub LLC ("Lody", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses,
stores, and protects information when you use our Services, including our website, web app,
desktop app, mobile apps, and CLI.

## 1. Data Controller and Contact

- Company: LoroHub LLC
- Email: contact@lody.ai

## 2. Information We Collect

Based on current product behavior, we collect the following categories of data:

### 2.1 Account and Authentication Data

- Account identifiers, profile basics, and email address provided via GitHub OAuth or email
  authentication
- Billing and subscription status metadata (payment card details are processed directly by Stripe
  and are not stored by Lody)

### 2.2 Device, Analytics, and Technical Data (Opt-In)

- When you explicitly allow usage sharing, we collect minimal product usage counts (such as
  sign-ups, message submission counts, coarse platform/OS, and built-in agent provider category)
  and redacted error categories. Conversation content, repository paths, URLs, page titles, custom
  agent names, and raw error stacks are excluded from product analytics.
- Product analytics resolves coarse country-level region before discarding raw client IP addresses
  prior to storage.
- On our public website, optional analytics cookies are disabled by default in the European Economic
  Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland until you accept via the Cookie Consent Banner. Visitors in
  other regions can disable optional analytics cookies anytime via **Cookie settings** in the
  website footer.

### 2.3 Service Logs

- Operational logs and security logs needed to run, debug, and secure the Services

### 2.4 User Content

- Conversation content and related workflow context you submit through the Services (workspaces
  with end-to-end encryption enabled keep conversation content encrypted on your devices)

## 3. Lawful Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process personal data under
the following lawful bases:

- **Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b))**: authenticating your account, providing core workspace
  and collaboration features, and managing paid subscriptions
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a))**: optional product usage data sharing and optional website analytics
  cookies; you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))**: securing the Services, preventing abuse, and diagnosing
  operational outages
- **Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c))**: tax, accounting, and compliance duties required by law

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

- **Essential cookies and storage**: required to maintain sign-in sessions, theme/language settings,
  and core app state.
- **Website analytics cookies (optional)**: disabled by default in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland
  until you accept in the Cookie Consent Banner (advertising and ad-personalization storage remain
  disabled everywhere). Visitors in all regions can change or disable optional analytics cookies
  anytime via **Cookie settings** in the website footer.
- **Product analytics storage**: Lody apps do not set product analytics cookies and do not
  initialize product analytics until you sign in and opt in.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Processing

We use trusted service providers (sub-processors) to run parts of the Services:

- Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare R2 (hosting and storage)
- Convex (backend infrastructure)
- GitHub (third-party login and repository integration)
- Stripe (subscription billing and payment processing)
- Google Analytics (optional public website analytics)
- PostHog (opt-in minimal product analytics and redacted error reporting)

These providers process data under their own terms, privacy policies, and applicable data
processing agreements (DPAs).

## 6. Cross-Border Transfer

Our cloud infrastructure and service providers (including PostHog, Convex, Cloudflare, Stripe, and
Google) may process data in the United States or other jurisdictions where they operate. Where
required by GDPR or other applicable laws, we rely on recognized transfer mechanisms such as
Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), vendor DPAs, and
strict data minimization controls.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Account and workspace data**: retained while your account remains active; permanently cleared
  within 30 days after a confirmed deletion request.
- **Product analytics (PostHog)**: minimal usage events are retained for up to 12 months (or
  deleted earlier upon account deletion request); raw client IP addresses are discarded before
  storage.
- **Operational and security logs**: retained for up to 90 days unless longer retention is required
  for security investigation or legal compliance.
- **Billing records**: retained as required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

You have the right to:

- **Control usage data sharing uniformly across your account**: choose whether to share usage data
  after your first sign-in on desktop, web, or mobile, change the unified account-level switch
  anytime in Settings → About → **Share app usage data** (applies across desktop, web, mobile,
  and synced CLI), or override CLI telemetry via `LODY_DISABLE_USAGE_SHARING=1` or `DO_NOT_TRACK=1`
- **Manage website cookies**: accept or reject optional analytics cookies via the Cookie Consent
  Banner or **Cookie settings** in the website footer
- **Access and export** a copy of your personal data (Art. 15 & Art. 20 data portability)
- **Rectify** inaccurate personal data (Art. 16)
- **Erase** your account and personal data (Art. 17)
- **Restrict or object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 18 & Art. 21)
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for consent-based processing (Art. 7(3))
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77)

To make a privacy request, use the [Account and Data Deletion page](/account-deletion) or email
contact@lody.ai.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information.
No system is perfectly secure, but we work to reduce risk and respond to incidents promptly.

## 10. Children and Minors

The Services are not directed to children under 16 years of age (or the minimum digital consent age
required in your jurisdiction, where higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from
them. If you believe a child has provided personal data without valid guardian consent, contact
contact@lody.ai so we can delete it.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and may provide additional
notice.

## 12. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact: contact@lody.ai
