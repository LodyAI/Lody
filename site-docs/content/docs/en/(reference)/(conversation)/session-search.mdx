# In-Conversation Search

In-conversation search helps you recover context from long sessions. It searches the
conversation text: your messages, the agent's replies, its thinking, and proposed plans —
including the progress text and thinking folded inside a finished turn's collapsed work
group.

Tool calls are not searchable. Tool titles, file paths, JSON input/output, terminal output,
and diffs stay out of the index so results only contain what was actually written in the
conversation.

## Open search

- Open the session menu and choose **Find in session**.
- On desktop, press `Ctrl+F`; on macOS, press `Cmd+F`.

The search box appears over the conversation without taking over the composer.

## Move through results

- Matching text is highlighted as you type.
- The counter shows the current result and total matches.
- Press `Enter` to jump to the next match.
- Press `Shift+Enter` or the up arrow to jump to the previous match.
- If nothing matches, the counter shows `0 / 0`.

The active result scrolls into view, expanding a collapsed work group or thinking card when
the match sits inside one.

## Clear or close

Press `Escape` once to clear the current query. Press `Escape` again to close search.
