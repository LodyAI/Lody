- Share conversations through read-only links with a static, fast-loading reader

- Navigate shared conversations more smoothly with clearer selection, sidebar controls, and in-memory history reuse
- Reduce sharing upload work and improve static share reliability
- Improve Streams token refresh and cache isolation across workspaces
- Restore spacing at the bottom of the composer
- Simplify sharing dialog copy and make shared pages more compact
