Version 0.89.2

### 🐛 Fixes and Improvements

- Fixed session tabs staying on the intended conversation and recovering correctly while asynchronous data or pending child sessions arrive, rapid switches occur, or sessions are restored.
- Reduced input latency during session switching by coalescing rapid navigation and moving background work out of the input path.
- Prevented malformed parent-child session relationships from causing redirect loops and renderer crashes.
